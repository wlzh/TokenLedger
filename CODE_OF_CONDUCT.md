# Community Conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Discuss technical decisions with evidence and allow disagreement without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, and disclosure of private employee or account data are not acceptable.

Maintainers should respond proportionately, explain moderation decisions when safe, and provide a way to appeal. Depending on severity, actions may include requesting edits, removing sensitive content, restricting participation, or exclusion.

The project maintainer and conduct coordinator is [@wlzh](https://github.com/wlzh). A private reporting channel and an alternate reviewer for complaints involving the maintainer must still be established before community operations begin. Until then, use an established private project-owner channel without posting identifying details publicly. A profile link does not imply a private messaging facility.

This is a project-specific draft, not a claim that a staffed enforcement process already exists.
